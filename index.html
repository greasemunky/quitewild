Your host

Meet Neel

For over a decade, the jungle has been my constant. Through a full-time job and life in the city, I kept finding my way back to Kabini and Nagarahole. I went on self-drive safaris and core-zone safaris, stayed in the forest, and spent more mornings than I can count behind a camera.

Three years ago, I started taking small groups along. Seeing someone spot their first tiger, or hearing the forest go quiet just before an alarm call, turned out to be the best part.

Now I do this full time, which means a few more slots each season. I take the photos, so you can simply be there.

How we safari

The forest comes first

  1. We keep our distance.No crowding a sighting, no pushing for a closer look.
  2. We stay quiet.Voices low in the jeep. You hear more, and so do they.
  3. We never chase.If an animal moves away, we let it go.
  4. We leave nothing behind.No plastic, no litter, no feeding, ever.
